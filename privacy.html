PocketPause

Privacy, without the fine-print theater.

This version of PocketPause has no account system, analytics service, advertising SDK, or cloud database.

What is stored

Your savings settings, moods, purchase contexts, decisions, reminder rules, and optional temptation-zone coordinates are stored in your browser’s local storage.

Location

Location access is optional and requested only after you press a location button. Coordinates are used on-device to compare your position with zones you saved. They are not transmitted by PocketPause.

Notifications

Browser notification permission is optional. This static PWA can remind you while it is active and can surface due cooling-off decisions. Reliable closed-app push notifications and continuous geofencing would require a native app or a push backend, neither of which is present here.

Your controls

You can export all app data as JSON or erase it from Settings. Clearing browser site data also removes it.

Financial information

PocketPause is an educational behavior-change tool. It does not connect to bank accounts, initiate transactions, or provide individualized financial, investment, tax, or legal advice.
